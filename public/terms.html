Terms of use

Last updated October 2026. Jjinja Battle is a free, just-for-fun hobby project.

Who can play

  • You must be at least 13 years old to use Jjinja Battle.
  • In random matches, only players who confirm they are 18 or older can turn their camera on. Don't lie about your age.

Be decent

Don't do any of the following, on camera, on the mic, or in your name:

  • nudity or sexual content,
  • harassment, threats, or hate toward anyone,
  • showing or sharing other people without their permission,
  • trying to break, overload, or cheat the game.

Players who are reported several times lose access to random matches. We may block anyone who breaks these rules.

Your camera and mic

Video and live audio travel directly between you and your opponent; see the privacy page for details. Anything you show on camera can be seen, and possibly recorded, by your opponent. Only turn your camera on if you're comfortable with that.

No guarantees

The game is provided as is. Scores are for fun, partly random, and not a real assessment of your Korean. The service may change, break, or shut down at any time.
